About FanFirst

Reclaiming live music for genuine fans.

The Origin Story

FanFirst was founded by Vipin Matlani. The idea was born in 2024 after missing out on tickets for Coldplay’s Music of the Spheres India Tour due to massive ticket scalping and automated bot traffic.

Frustrated by unfair access and inflated resale pricing, Vipin set out to build a platform where behavioral fan engagement determines ticket priority—ensuring real supporters get access at true face value.
